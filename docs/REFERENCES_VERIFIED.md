# Verified references

Every entry below was confirmed on 2026-10-18 by fetching a primary record
(Crossref by DOI, OpenAlex by DOI, the NBER page, sec.gov, census.gov, or an
author-hosted PDF). Summaries are written from the fetched abstract or document
only. Nothing in the paper's bibliography is cited from memory. Items that could
not be verified are listed at the end and are not cited.

## Textual analysis of filings and boilerplate

- Loughran, T., and McDonald, B. (2011). When is a liability not a liability?
  Textual analysis, dictionaries, and 10-Ks. *Journal of Finance* 66(1), 35–65.
  doi:10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01625.x
- Loughran, T., and McDonald, B. (2016). Textual analysis in accounting and
  finance: a survey. *Journal of Accounting Research* 54(4), 1187–1230.
  doi:10.1111/1475-679X.12123
- Dyer, T., Lang, M., and Stice-Lawrence, L. (2017). The evolution of 10-K
  textual disclosure: evidence from Latent Dirichlet Allocation. *Journal of
  Accounting and Economics* 64(2–3), 221–245. doi:10.1016/j.jacceco.2017.07.002.
  Verified: abstract (length, boilerplate, stickiness and redundancy rise 1996 to
  2013; three topics, fair value, internal controls and risk factors, account for
  virtually all of the length increase). Not verified: the exact n-gram definition
  of their boilerplate measure, so the paper does not state it.
- Lang, M., and Stice-Lawrence, L. (2015). Textual analysis and international
  financial reporting: large sample evidence. *Journal of Accounting and Economics*
  60(2–3), 110–135. doi:10.1016/j.jacceco.2015.09.002. Verified from the
  working-paper PDF: boilerplate is measured from four-word phrases (tetragrams)
  that are common across a country's filings, with sentences flagged when they
  contain a tetragram that appears in a large share of documents.
- Cazier, R. A., and Pfeiffer, R. J. (2016). Why are 10-K filings so long?
  *Accounting Horizons* 30(1), 1–21. doi:10.2308/acch-51240
- Brown, S. V., and Tucker, J. W. (2011). Large-sample evidence on firms'
  year-over-year MD&A modifications. *Journal of Accounting Research* 49(2),
  309–346. doi:10.1111/j.1475-679X.2010.00396.x
- Hoberg, G., and Phillips, G. (2016). Text-based network industries and
  endogenous product differentiation. *Journal of Political Economy* 124(5),
  1423–1465. doi:10.1086/688176
