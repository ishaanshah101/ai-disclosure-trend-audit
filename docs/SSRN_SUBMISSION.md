# SSRN submission package

Everything needed to post the paper, ready to paste. Posting takes about three
minutes once signed in at https://papers.ssrn.com (My Papers, Submit a Paper). SSRN
asks for the PDF, the fields below, and a classification; no endorsement is needed.

**File to upload:** `paper/ai-disclosure-trend-audit.pdf`

**Title**

The Boilerplate Decade: How Much of the Rise in Artificial-Intelligence Language in
Annual Reports Is Substantive, 2015 to 2026

**Author:** Ishaan Shah, The Athenian School, Danville, California

**Abstract** (use the paper's abstract verbatim; it is under SSRN's limit)

**Keywords**

artificial intelligence; corporate disclosure; 10-K; textual analysis; boilerplate;
risk factors; AI adoption measurement; preregistration; AI washing

**JEL codes**

G38 (Government Policy and Regulation), M41 (Accounting), M48 (Government Policy and
Regulation, Accounting), O33 (Technological Change: Choices and Consequences),
C81 (Methodology for Collecting, Estimating, and Organizing Microeconomic Data)

**SSRN networks to select**

Financial Economics Network (FEN); Accounting Research Network (ARN); Economics
Research Network (ERN). Suggested eJournals: Corporate Governance & Finance;
Financial Accounting; Information Systems & Economics; Econometrics: Data Collection
& Data Estimation Methodology.

**Data and code statement** (paste in the "Data" or "Notes" field)

All data, code, the preregistration with its dated amendments, the literal rater
prompt, every sentence and every judgement are at
https://github.com/ishaanshah101/ai-disclosure-trend-audit (MIT licence). A
companion page lets readers filter and read every sentence with its verdicts.

**Other venues that take this paper without endorsement**

- OSF Preprints (osf.io/preprints): same package, choose the Social and Behavioral
  Sciences, Economics subject.
- Journal of Student Research (jsr.org): high-school research track; the paper is
  within its length limit with the references counted.
- arXiv econ.GN requires an endorser; Prof. Bojić or any arXiv-registered economist
  can endorse with one click once the account exists.

**Before posting**

- Make the GitHub repository public (Settings, General, Danger Zone, Change
  visibility) so the data link resolves.
- Optionally create a Zenodo DOI: sign in at zenodo.org with GitHub, enable the
  repository under GitHub integration, then create a release v1.0.0 on GitHub; Zenodo
  mints the DOI automatically. Add the DOI to CITATION.cff and the README afterwards.
